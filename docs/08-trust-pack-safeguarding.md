## Safeguarding policy outline
**Principles**
- Welfare of the child/vulnerable adult is paramount
- Two-adult rule: never one adult alone with a minor; no 1:1 closed-door situations
- All adults in contact with minors are background-checked and trained

**Prevention**
- Guardian consent + ID verification at booking; named portal contacts only
- Risk labels on activities; semi-risky = enhanced safeguards
- Phone custody protocol removes the main exploitation/leak vector (personal phone)
- Portal shows city/venue only; audit-logged access

**Reporting flow**
1. Any concern (staff, participant, guardian, portal message) → Designated Safeguarding Lead (DSL) immediately
2. DSL assesses; if risk of harm → authorities (BR: 190; Conselho Tutelar for minors) and insurer notified same day
3. Session suspended if needed; participant/family contacted via portal protocol
4. Written incident report within 24h; preserved logs; review by platform admin
5. No retaliation for good-faith reports

**Records & review**
- All safeguarding records access-restricted, retained per legal requirement
- Policy reviewed annually and after every incident
- Training: safeguarding + first aid + checkpoint protocol, refreshed annually
