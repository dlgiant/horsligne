# Provider / Facilitator Onboarding Checklist

Applies to facilitators (meditation, hikes, workshops) and activity providers (surf, paddle, trail tours). Status workflow: applied → documents → interview → vetted → live.

## 1. Identity & business
- [ ] Full legal name, government ID verified
- [ ] Business registration (CNPJ or local equivalent) if operating as a business
- [ ] Contact phone + email; emergency contact

## 2. Background checks (mandatory)
- [ ] Criminal background check (Brazil: certidão de antecedentes criminais; abroad: DBS or local equivalent)
- [ ] Required for ALL providers; refreshed annually
- [ ] Enhanced check + safeguarding training if working with minors

## 3. Insurance
- [ ] Public liability insurance (min coverage per platform standard)
- [ ] Professional indemnity insurance
- [ ] Abuse/molestation coverage — mandatory if minors may participate
- [ ] Certificates uploaded; expiry dates tracked; auto-reminder at 30/7 days

## 4. Competence & safety
- [ ] First-aid certification (wilderness first aid for remote venues)
- [ ] Satellite communicator training for remote venues (inReach SOS drill)
- [ ] Two sample-session review by platform (or observed live session)
- [ ] Two references checked
- [ ] Venue emergency plan reviewed: evacuation route, 190/192 or local equivalents, nearest hospital

## 5. Activity listing requirements
- [ ] Name, description, duration, capacity, price, meeting point (city-level)
- [ ] Risk label: low / moderate / semi-risky — with justification; semi-risky requires extra safeguards
- [ ] Equipment and inclusions listed; what participant must bring
- [ ] Cancellation policy
- [ ] Own waiver per activity, signed at booking

## 6. Terms (signed before going live)
- [ ] Revenue share: platform takes 15–20% per booking
- [ ] Payout schedule (weekly, minus refunds)
- [ ] Code of conduct (professional boundaries, no 1:1 with minors without guardian, alcohol/drugs zero tolerance)
- [ ] Safeguarding training completed
- [ ] Duty to report incidents within 24h via admin console
- [ ] Phone-custody protocol: phones in sealed pouch / accommodation safe during their activity
- [ ] Checkpoint duty: log all inbound messages, follow verification protocol, never interrupt for unverified messages

## 7. Ongoing
- [ ] Rating ≥ 4.0 maintained; below → review
- [ ] Annual re-vetting (background check + insurance renewal)
- [ ] Immediate suspension on any safeguarding concern, pending investigation
