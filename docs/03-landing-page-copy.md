### The problem
Traveling with your phone out is a theft risk. Traveling without it makes your family anxious. We built the middle path: you go phone-free, they watch a calm status page.

### How it works (3 steps)
1. **Book & prepay** in the app while you have wifi — sessions, retreats, guided activities in the area you're visiting.
2. **Leave your phone safe** — at your accommodation or in our sealed pouch at the venue. Checked in by a vetted local facilitator.
3. **Live the moment** — zero bars needed. At check-out you get a summary: phone-free time, and any verified messages from outside.

### For families: the portal
Your loved one shares a unique link + password + one-time code with you. From any browser you see: live session status in your timezone and theirs, expected check-out (with an overdue alert and the venue's emergency number if it runs late), and a way to send routine or urgent messages. Urgent messages are verified by callback before anyone interrupts. After check-out you see the full summary. You never see their exact location — venue and city only.

### Gift an experience
Browse "What's on in [destination]" in the portal and book a paddleboard session, surf lesson or trail tour as a gift. They get a notification, accept or decline — their call, always. Every activity shows a risk label and a vetted, insured local guide.

### Safety & trust
- Facilitators are background-checked, first-aid trained; two-adult rule for minors
- Insurance: public liability, professional indemnity, abuse cover for youth activities
- Remote venues carry a satellite SOS communicator; every session listing shows its emergency channel
- LGPD/GDPR compliant; per-trip credentials expire automatically; full audit log
- Signed waiver + guardian consent for minors at booking

### FAQ
**What if there's an emergency and there's no signal?** Every venue has a satellite SOS or landline; the portal shows which, per session. We don't run sessions where there's no emergency channel.
**Who can see my location?** No one. The portal shows venue + city only.
**What if I don't check out on time?** After a 30-minute grace, your family sees "overdue" with the venue's direct number. Overdue ≠ emergency.
**Can my family book things for me?** Yes — as gifts or proposals. You always accept or decline.
**What happens to messages sent during a session?** Logged, verified if marked urgent, and relayed to you at check-out on screen, on a printed slip, and by SMS once you're back in coverage.
