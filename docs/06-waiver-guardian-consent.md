# Waiver & Guardian Consent — Clause Outline

**Draft for legal counsel review before use.** Version and timestamp every signature; store immutably.

## A. Participant waiver (adults)
1. Voluntary participation; right to withdraw at any time
2. Phone custody: phone stays at the participant's chosen safe place (accommodation) or in the venue's sealed pouch with claim code; the venue/platform is not liable for the phone while outside venue custody
3. Assumption of risk, matched to the activity's risk label (low / moderate / semi-risky); semi-risky activities require full safeguards (two-adult rule, satellite/landline confirmed)
4. Medical disclosure: participant confirms fitness or discloses conditions; emergency treatment consent (facilitator may call emergency services and administer first aid)
5. Emergency contact: name, phone, relation — mandatory
6. Media/photo consent: optional, separately ticked
7. Cancellation & refund policy (full refund ≥ 24h before; no-show fee after)
8. Liability waiver and indemnity (to the extent permitted by law)
9. Dispute resolution: mediation → arbitration; governing law: [jurisdiction]
10. Data protection consent (LGPD/GDPR): purposes (booking, safety, checkpoint relay, portal), retention (trip end + 24h; logs 12 months), rights (access/correction/deletion), DPO contact
11. Acknowledgment: "I understand no one can reach me by phone during the session except through the verified checkpoint protocol."

## B. Guardian consent (minors)
All of A plus:
1. Guardian identity, relationship, and ID verification
2. Explicit consent to: participation, phone custody arrangement, sharing of medical info with facilitators/emergency services
3. Named portal contacts (guardians only) + OTP to guardian's own device
4. Acknowledgment of background-check process and two-adult rule; no 1:1 facilitator–minor situations without guardian present
5. Pickup/check-out authorization: who may collect the minor; check-out summary shared with guardian via portal
6. Consent for photographs only if separately ticked

## C. Signature mechanics
- Typed full name + timestamp (UTC) + IP + document version hash
- Stored append-only; copy emailed to participant/guardian
- Re-sign required on any material change (new risk label, new venue type)
